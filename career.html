Frequently Asked Questions

What does the application process look like?

Our process is straightforward: an initial application review, a 30-minute intro call with our People team, a take-home task relevant to your role, and a final panel interview with the team. We aim to complete the process within 3 weeks and give feedback at every stage.

Are all roles fully remote?

Most of our roles are remote-first. Some positions are listed as Hybrid, which means occasional visits to our Jakarta or Singapore offices. Each job listing specifies the work arrangement clearly.

Do I need a formal degree to apply?

No. We evaluate candidates on skills, portfolio, and demonstrated ability — not degrees. We actively welcome people who are self-taught, graduates, or career changers. What matters is what you can do.
